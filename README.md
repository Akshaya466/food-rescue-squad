Annora — Surplus Food Redistribution

**Share More. Waste Less. Feed More.**

Annora is a web application that helps reduce food waste by connecting people and organizations with surplus food to those who need it. It provides a shared platform for food donors, collectors, and delivery volunteers to coordinate food redistribution.

##  The Problem

Restaurants, hotels, canteens, and other food providers may have surplus food that remains unused. Meanwhile, people and organizations supporting those in need may struggle to access food.

Annora aims to bridge this gap by making surplus-food listings easier to share, discover, and coordinate for redistribution.

## Solution

Annora brings together three key roles in one platform:

###  Donors
Food donors can register, post details about their available surplus food, and manage their own listings. This helps make usable surplus food visible to authorized collectors.

### Collectors
Collectors can explore available food listings, review relevant details, and claim eligible listings through the platform.

### Delivery Volunteers
Delivery volunteers can access the delivery-related features and claim alerts available to their role, helping coordinate the movement of food to its intended destination.

## Key Features

- **Role-based registration:** Users choose whether they want to register as a Donor, Collector, or Delivery Volunteer.
- **Authentication:** Supports email-and-password registration and Google sign-in.
- **Food listing management:** Donors can create and manage their own surplus-food listings.
- **Food discovery:** Collectors can view available food listings.
- **Claim workflow:** Collectors can claim eligible food listings.
- **Delivery coordination:** Volunteers have access to authorized delivery-related functionality.
- **Role-based access control:** Users receive permissions appropriate to their roles.
- **Cloud-based data storage:** Firebase stores application data and supports the platform's authentication and access-control workflows.

## How Annora Works

1. **Register:** Create an account and select a role.
2. **Donate:** A donor posts details about surplus food.
3. **Discover:** A collector browses the available listings.
4. **Claim:** The collector claims an eligible listing.
5. **Coordinate delivery:** Delivery volunteers use the available delivery workflow to help coordinate delivery.

The exact delivery and notification steps depend on the features enabled in the deployed application.

## Mission

Annora aims to make food redistribution more accessible and coordinated by helping surplus food reach people and organizations that can use it.

The project supports the goals of **UN Sustainable Development Goal 2 — Zero Hunger** and **SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production**.
